about me

My love for reading started a few years ago when I picked up my first adventure novel out of curiosity. What began as a simple way to spend a rainy afternoon soon turned into a daily habit. Whenever I find some free time away from school or work, I open a book and let myself slip into a completely different world. Whether it is a thrilling mystery, a historical biography, or a science fiction story, each page offers a new adventure.
